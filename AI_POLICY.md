# AI Usage Policy

__How you work is up to you__: It is not an objective of this document to either encourage or discourage AI use in your work process.

With that in mind, below are some rules that I'd like to enforce in this repository.

* __No automated commits, pull requests or submissions__: This means no agents or any other automation interactinv with this repository directly.
* __The human contributor must agree to bear  ultimate responsibility for the contribution__: The contributor must understand, own and stand behind their contribution, be able to answer questions and discuss tradeoffs.
* __Any contributed code must be manually tested by the human contributor__: Otherwise, that task falls on the maintainer and becomes a forced burden rather than a positive action.
* __Prose (documentation, comments, and so on) must be either authored or heavily curated and edited by the human contributor__: Prose written by LLM is severely lacking in quality and is borderline unreadable. This repository should be easy to use and simple to understand and therefore a high bar of readability is demanded of any prose.
* __Contributions must be scoped to one issue at a time__: It is quite easy for LLM to generate thousands of lines of code, making it impossible to properly reason about and discuss the contribution.
* __No generative art, audio, images or any other non text media__

__Any contribution that does not abide by the above will be instantly rejected.__

## Rationale behind the policy
I'm not against AI usage. In fact, I use AI myself for various aspects of my work in many capacities. 

As of the time of writing this, in 2026, I'm just one guy, and I have no time or resources to review fully automated pull requests.
